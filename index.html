Questions

Do I need WhatsApp Business?
No. The button opens a wa.me link, which works with any number that has WhatsApp, on the regular app or the Business app.
What happens when someone clicks it?
On a phone, WhatsApp opens with your chat and the message ready to send. On a computer, WhatsApp Web or the desktop app opens.
Does it track visitors?
No. No cookies, no storage, no requests of its own. It only builds a link.
